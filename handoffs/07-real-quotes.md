## Inference
- The exact price arrives with the quote. Armut-style "range first, pro prices it" is how the model works, so the funnel job before the quote is getting the couple to submit.
- Strong pros sell on package detail and portfolio links. The platform does not need to show them; it could nudge weaker pros to do the same.
- Contact leaves the platform quickly (WhatsApp, phone). Measuring hires from quotes needs a signal from the platform or a follow-up question.
