## A hidden bridge: çeyiz taşıma
- Fact: 45,882 reviews (4.9). Price page https://armut.com/fiyatlari/istanbul-ceyiz-tasima_77420_34 shows 15 requests 19 Aug to 15 Oct 2026; several go to another city (Muğla, Adana, Balıkesir, Ordu, Düzce, Gaziosmanpaşa).
- Inference: almost every çeyiz requester is a couple close to the wedding, already trusting Armut for an Armut-style job. The wedding may be in the destination city.

## Guarantee
- Fact: https://armut.com/garanti covers property damage and injury up to 2,000 TL if the quote is chosen on Armut (page footer says 2011-2014, may be old). It does not mention no-show or late delivery, which are the main event fears (03 T1, T2).
