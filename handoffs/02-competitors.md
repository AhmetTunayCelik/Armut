## Competitors
**Dugun.com (Turkey, main rival for wedding demand)**
- Fact: vendor directory, free couple accounts, planner. Couples request quotes from partner firms; firms pay memberships. https://webrazzi.com/2013/06/14/dugun-com/ (2013, dated)
- Fact (company claim): 20,000+ firms, about 300,000 couples a year (https://finans.mynet.com/haber/detay/ekonomi/dugun-com-her-yil-evlilik-hazirligindaki-300-bin-cifte-ulasiyor/156534/ , 2018); later 40,000+ firms, 36 categories, 40+ cities (https://www.capital.com.tr/haberler/tum-haberler/duguncomdan-yurtdisi-basarisi , 2020-02-25).
- Fact (CEO statement, 2018): couples start with the venue, then contact venues in budget.
- Inference: strength is wedding depth and a long head start; weakness is weddings only.
- Fact: Sikayetvar lists 224 complaints (vendor calls, quote mismatch, cancellation). https://www.sikayetvar.com/duguncom (undated; not representative)

**DugunBuketi.com (Turkey)**
- Fact: founded 2016 in Izmir; free planning consultation; bought DugunYardimcisi.com and Ceyiz Listem app (150,000+ members). https://webrazzi.com/2020/07/14/dugunbuketicom-ceyiz-listem-ve-dugunyardimcisicomu-satin-aldi (2020-07-14)
- Fact (company claim): about 500,000 monthly visitors. Revenue model not stated.
- Inference: human-assisted consultation is a manual form of bundling.

**FotografPaketi.com (niche, thin evidence)**
- Fact: 2017 Webrazzi title describes it as quotes from photographers (page not read). https://webrazzi.com/2017/02/28/fotografpaketi-com-profesyonel-fotografcilardan-teklif-almanin-kolay-yolu/
- Assumption: may be inactive. Confirm by opening the site.

**Benchmarks only**
- The Knot (US): browse by category and city, filter by price and distance, reviews, then Request Quote. https://helpcenter.theknot.com/hc/en-us/articles/46635549990548-How-do-I-find-local-wedding-vendors (undated). Inference: browse-first with visible prices, opposite of Armut's question-first.
- Bark: customers answer questions, pros pay credits to contact a lead. https://help.bark.com/hc/en-au/articles/13342669635484-What-is-Bark-and-how-does-it-work (undated)
- Thumbtack: pay per lead, reportedly about $10 to over $100 (third-party). https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/what-is-thumbtack-how-it-works/
- Inference: Bark and Thumbtack are the closest model to Armut.

## So what
- Implication (medium): compete on speed to a price, not directory depth.
- Implication (medium): a multi-service wedding request could be an entry point nobody clearly offers (low confidence that nobody does).
- Implication (low): trust is open ground given Dugun.com complaints.
- Risk: venue-led and Instagram-led booking may bypass platforms (medium). Dugun.com owns the planning mindset and SEO (medium).
- Opportunity: birthday cake and non-wedding events sit outside Dugun.com's core (low-medium).
- Assumptions to validate: (1) couples request 2+ services but Armut treats them separately (funnel data); (2) couples book venue first so the date can be captured early (5-8 interviews); (3) price opacity before the quote is the main drop-off (step funnel).
