# 12 Ideas round 3: awareness and trust (Product Lead + agents), 2026-11-07
New framing (Bulent): events are not "Armut style"; people may not know Armut offers them, or may not trust the quality.
Evidence: handoffs/11 (Armut first-hand), awareness/trust agent report (examples below), 09 and 10.
Constraints unchanged: no shared-flow change, no pricing change, event-only or CRM/marketing/merchandising/partner/supply layers, small team.

## What the evidence says
- Quality exists but is invisible: Istanbul event services rated 4.8-4.9 vs 4.1 cleaning, 4.4 moving; about 17,000 event reviews (Fact, 11).
- Armut already has wedding customers who don't know it: çeyiz taşıma, 45,882 reviews, 4.9 (Fact, 11). Couples near their wedding, trusting Armut for an Armut-style job.
- The guarantee covers damage up to 2,000 TL, not no-show (Fact). Thumbtack covers "job not done" up to $2,500 (Fact, thumbtack.com/guarantee).
- Cross-category moments work for others: Uber Eats emails after an airport ride (Fact, pymnts); Uber says multi-product users retain 35% better (company claim, emarketer). No published lift for services marketplaces (Gap).

## Proposed first change (one build, three entry points)
"Next for your wedding" card after submit. Shown on success page, tracker and follow-up email for:
1. çeyiz taşıma and çeyiz nakliye (bridge from core users: awareness),
2. gelin arabası süsleme and wedding event requests (same-event cross-sell),
3. birthday cake to decoration/organization (same party).
Card content: 2-3 event services, city (destination city for çeyiz), Armut's real rating and review count (trust), one tap into each existing flow. No change to the flow or prices.
Test: randomize by user 50/50 among submitters of those services. Primary: event requests within 14 days per submitter. Guardrails: hire rate and quotes per request on card-driven vs organic requests; pro complaints; core category metrics unchanged (card is after submit).
Parallel, no build: A2 seasonal CRM with proof to a past-customer segment, 20% holdout.
Next: T2 event-day backup (ops pilot, wedding photographer, Istanbul).
