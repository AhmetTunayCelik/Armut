## Channel preference (Event Industry Expert, 8 searches)
- Fact: home and small-workshop bakers find customers mainly through Instagram, their storefront and order channel (Bigpara/Hürriyet 2026-08-17, Istanbul pastry chamber; growth "not yet measurable").
- Fact: İSFO president: couples find photographers online; all chamber complaints involve photographers found online (Milliyet/AA 2026-03-23). Couples report prepaid albums never delivered via Instagram vendors (İşçi Haber 2026-07-05; anecdotes).
- Fact: some wedding halls allow only their contracted photographer (Türkiye Gazetesi 2018, one old case).
- Fact (company claim, 2020): düğün.com says ~400k of ~550k couples a year use it (venue-focused, self-reported).
- Fact: Armut kına price page lists 700 kına firms in Istanbul, real requests and reviews (checked 2026-11-08).
- Gap: no independent survey of channel shares.
Verdict (Inference): medium overall. Instagram leads discovery, not necessarily comparison and booking; its trust gap (prepayment fraud) is room for a quote marketplace. By service: butik pasta strong (Instagram is the shop), bridal make-up medium-strong (assumption), wedding photographer medium, kına/nişan organization weak-medium.
Implications: (1) D1 should lead with photographer and organization services; cake is a test, not the lead. (2) Work with Instagram, not against it: pro share link (pros put an Armut request link in their Instagram bio or DMs; origin tagged via a short redirect; metric requests per active event pro; guardrail hire rate vs organic) and venue/kına-salon QR partnerships. Both were roadmap items; the pro share link moves to "next after P1" because it targets exactly the channel-preference segment.
Pre-test (from handoff 27) now also asks: "where did you look first?" in 5 interviews, and splits tile CTR by service.
