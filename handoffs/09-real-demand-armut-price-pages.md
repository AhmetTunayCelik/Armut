# 09 Real event demand on Armut (first-hand), checked 2026-11-07
Source: Armut public price pages, which show sample real requests with their free text. Caveat: Armut chooses which examples to show (selection bias). Only Istanbul checked.

## Wedding photographer, Istanbul
https://armut.com/fiyatlari/istanbul-dugun-fotografcisi_194_34 . Average range shown 4.000-40.000 TL. 20 requests, 18 Aug to 24 Oct 2026.
- Fact: 13 of 20 state an event date in the free text. Days from request to event: 1,1,1,1,2,5,7,11,16,21,27,30,38. Median 7. 10 of 13 within 3 weeks. None over 6 weeks.
- Fact: many are small or partial events: nikah, takı töreni, nikah yemeği, kına, gelin alma, pre-wedding shoot, maternity shoot. Hours mostly 0-4. Accepted prices 2.000-15.000 TL.
- Fact: one request asks for kına and düğün together; one says "we already have a photographer, only video and drone".
- Fact: the page lists separate event services Armut already has: Nişan, Söz, Nikah, Kına, Sünnet, Evlilik Teklifi, Doğum Günü, Hamile, Yenidoğan fotoğrafçısı.
- Inference: Armut's current wedding demand is short-notice, small, budget events. The free-text step is where the date lives, which supports Armut keeping it mandatory. My earlier H7 ("within three weeks" default mismatches weddings) is weakened for current users; it fits them. Assumption until checked on all requests.

## Bridal make-up, Istanbul
https://armut.com/fiyatlari/istanbul-gelin-makyaji_42395_34
- Fact: only 6 examples (Apr-Oct 2026). Lead times where stated: 19, 20, 24 days. 755 pros listed, 4.8 rating from 884 reviews. Options include Düğün, Kına, Nikah.
- Inference: supply and reviews are strong but visible demand is thin.

## Birthday cake, Istanbul
https://armut.com/fiyatlari/istanbul-dogum-gunu-pastasi_466_34
- Fact: 17+ requests in two weeks (11-25 Oct 2026). Mostly 6-10 people, 1.400-8.000 TL. Design-led (people say they will send an image). For partners, friends, children (one 14th birthday, one for a new father). One gives a date 4 days ahead.
- Inference: frequent, small, near-date, and tied to a date that repeats every year.
