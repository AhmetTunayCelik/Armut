## One platform underneath (Inference, confirm with the team)
- Request numbers created minutes apart are consecutive (Armut 57338138, ProntoPro 57338238, Homerun 57338294): shared job system.

## What changes in the recommendation
- Solutions are platform layers: occasion entry + grouped occasion page, post-submit "what happens next" + next-service card, SEO page upgrade, creator program. Build once, roll out per market.
- Occasion content per market: TR söz/nişan/kına/düğün, doğum günü; IT matrimonio, comunione/battesimo, compleanno; UK wedding, birthday, hen party (Assumption: confirm local demand).
- Test plan: start in one market with the strongest evidence (TR) and one ProntoPro market as replication; ProntoPro's existing trending tile is the baseline for the occasion entry.
- Numbers on slides: Armut figures labelled "Armut (TR) public estimates"; no platform-wide totals until ProntoPro/Homerun data is pulled.
- First data ask now platform-wide: event funnel and 48h quote rate per market.
