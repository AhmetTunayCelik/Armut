## Need themes
Almost all wedding-vendor complaints found are about a different business (DugunumuzVar), not Armut. No Armut wedding, makeup or cake complaint found.
- T1 Trust that the portfolio matches reality. Recurring (2 complaints, one competitor). Fact: bride paid 15,000 TL for photo and makeup, says it fell short of the portfolio. https://www.sikayetvar.com/dugunumuzvar/dugun-fotografi-ve-makyaj-hizmetinde-aldatici-reklam-kalitesiz-cekimler-ve-ucret-iadesi-talebi (undated). Inference: people need proof of real work.
- T2 Certainty the deliverable arrives. Recurring (2 threads plus news). Fact: album and video undelivered four months after a 16 September shoot, complaint posted 14 January. https://www.sikayetvar.com/dugunumuzvar/dugunumuzvar-beykozda-fotografcinin-teslimat-gecikmesi-ve-ucret-iadesi-talebi . Fact: Milliyet 2026-03-23, the Istanbul photographers' chamber president (ISFO) calls unregistered online photographers the biggest problem. https://www.milliyet.com.tr/haber/7559069/ . Inference: need is risk reduction. Armut has a guarantee page (https://armut.com/GuaranteeTerms.aspx, title only, event coverage unchecked).
- T3 Clear price before commit. Isolated in the voice. Inference only: wide ranges and extras suggest customers cannot tell what drives price. Needs interviews.
- T4 Providers doubt lead quality (supply side, not event-specific). Fact: Sikayetvar Armut page 47/100, 1,439 reviews, 10,908 complaints (2026-11-07). https://www.sikayetvar.com/armut . Fact: a painter paid for quotes, 12 July 2025, customers unreachable. https://www.sikayetvar.com/armut/teklif-verdigim-musteriler-ulasilmiyor-kesilen-ucretin-iadesini-talep-ediyorum . Fact: musicians, DJs and event organizers appear among bid-fee complaints. Inference: more event requests will not help if providers feel they pay for dead leads. Small sample.
- T5 Compare a few vendors with photos and reviews, then ask for quotes. Fact (venues, 2020): couples filter, review about 10, narrow to 5, request quotes. https://webrazzi.com/2020/07/28/dijitallesen-dugun-sektorunun-pandemi-donemi-ve-sonrasi/ . Dated and about venues, not photographers.

## Demand and seasonality
- Fact: 552,237 marriages in 2025, 569,983 in 2024 (about -3.1%). https://www.dha.com.tr/gundem/2025te-552-bin-cift-evlendi-193-bin-cift-bosandi-2824953 (2026-02-24)
- Fact: first-marriage age 28.5 men, 26.0 women in 2025. https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/02/24/evlenme-ve-bosanma-istatistikleri-2025/
- Fact (2019 data, indicative): July and August 12.4% each, September 12.3%; February lowest. https://www.alomaliye.com/2020/03/09/en-populer-evlenme-tarihi/
- Fact (2020 article, venues): about two-thirds of weddings in summer; venues book months ahead. Webrazzi link above.
- Fact: Istanbul average wedding cost 850,095 TL (salon) or 981,895 TL (outdoor) in 2025, no photographer line. https://www.haberturk.com/istanbul-da-evlenmenin-maliyeti-850000-tl-3816714-ekonomi (2025-08-22)
- Inference: about 550,000 couples a year, flat to falling, so growth must come from share. Booking window likely opens winter and spring before the summer peak.

## Price benchmarks
- Fact: Istanbul shoots start about 12,000-13,000 TL (Milliyet 2026-03-23, quoting ISFO president, link above).
- Fact: Samsun starter 5,000-8,500 TL, comprehensive 12,000-20,000 TL (Haber Gazetesi, 2026-01-10, no named vendors). https://www.habergazetesi.com.tr/samsunda-dugun-fotografcisi-paketleri-fiyatlari-kac-para
- Fact: complainants paid 24,000 TL (photo and album) and 15,000 TL (photo and makeup) at DugunumuzVar (undated).
- Inference: Armut's 5,000-30,000 TL start range looks wide but plausible. Its 5,000-12,000 TL full-day range may look low for Istanbul. Assumption: could attract price-led requests and under-set expectations. Test with real quotes (our 3 live requests may help).
- Gap: no citable makeup or cake prices.

## Switching triggers
- Away from a vendor: late or missing album, work below portfolio (refund demands).
- Toward a platform (Inference): someone vets the vendor and protects the deposit.
- Armut's own weak spots in the voice are provider-side.
- Fact (title only, not opened): Armut has combined photo + makeup pages, e.g. https://armut.com/pendik-fotograf-cekimi-makyaji . Relevant to H5; open it by hand.

## So what
Opportunity hypotheses written as problems:
- P1 Couples who plan weeks or months ahead meet a flow built for urgent home services, so event requests stall (H1, H4, H7).
- P2 Couples cannot judge a vendor's real work or price before asking for quotes, so the first step feels risky (H2, T1, T3).
- P3 Event providers doubt lead quality, which may limit supply and quote speed in peak months (T4, H3).
Interview assumptions (5-8 interviews):
- A1 Most couples hire photographer, makeup and cake separately and would use one place for all (H5).
- A2 Couples want full galleries and a price range before requesting (H2, T3).
- A3 Couples fear delivery risk more than price; vetting or deposit protection would change their choice (T2).
