Brief for brainstorm agents (2026-11-07)
Case: APM case study for Armut (Turkey services marketplace). Goal: more requests for event services (wedding photographer, bridal make-up, birthday cake, kına and birthday organization, etc.).
How Armut works: customer answers short questions plus a mandatory free-text step, gets up to 5 quotes (price, reviews, contact, message), then agrees with the pro by message or phone. Armut knows the hire only if the customer presses "Teklifi Seç". Armut Garantisi covers damage up to 2,000 TL, not no-show.
Hard constraints (head of product): no change to the shared request flow for all categories; no change to pricing (range shown, pro sets exact price); event-only, or CRM / marketing / merchandising / partnership / supply / content layers; team 1 BE, 1 FE, 1 UX; first test buildable in weeks; measurable.
Key facts: events are "not Armut style": people may not know Armut offers them or trust the quality. Customers a year (Armut pro pages): moving 186,667; cleaning 84,391; birthday cake 8,963; furniture assembly 8,376; wedding photographer 2,947; çeyiz taşıma 1,522. 552,237 marriages in 2025, so wedding photographer is ~0.5%. Istanbul wedding photographer requests are short-notice (median 7 days in 13 published examples) and small (nikah, takı töreni, kına, gelin alma, 0-4 h, 2,000-15,000 TL). Birthday cake requests are frequent, small, design-led, close to the date. Event pages show ratings 4.8-4.9 vs 4.1 cleaning (review counts pooled, scope unknown). Istanbul lists 937 wedding photographers. Homepage shows no event service. After submit, no cross-sell. About 40% of requests come from apps.
Approved first change: "Next for your wedding/party" card after submit (start with birthday cake, then wedding, çeyiz), one tap into existing flows, with rating shown.
Ideas already on the list (do not repeat unless you improve them): çeyiz bridge, same-event cross-sell, birthday comeback reminder, show ratings, seasonal homepage slot, seasonal CRM, pro Instagram share link, intent paid campaigns, occasion hubs, event-day backup (roadmap), curated pros, no-show money-back (roadmap), Ministry e-Devlet couples code (roadmap), "did you pick your pro?" Teklifi Seç reminder, post-event photo review, pro referral link, keyword bridge from moving.
